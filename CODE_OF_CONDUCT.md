# Code of Conduct

We welcome participation regardless of background, identity or experience.
Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers enforce this policy fairly and may remove inappropriate content,
reject contributions or restrict participation. Reports are handled with
respect for the privacy of those involved. For a private report, use the contact
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify it as a conduct concern.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces,
as well as when someone officially represents the project in public.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
